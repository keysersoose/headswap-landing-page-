FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Headloom?+

Headloom is an AI head-replacement workflow for catalogue and marketing teams that need controlled talent swaps without re-shooting. It is designed to preserve lighting, composition, and fine detail.

How fast can a project turn around?+

Turnaround depends on shot count, resolution, review cycles, and deployment model. We scope a reviewable pilot first, then agree production service levels around the approved workflow.

Do we retain control over likeness rights?+

Yes. Work is performed only against approved assets and applicable likeness permissions. Commercial terms, usage rights, retention, and audit requirements are agreed before production.
