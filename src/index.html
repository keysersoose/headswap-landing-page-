What we are building on Claude · agentic refinement

Claude refines the garment in every AI image.

Headloom makes AI-generated fashion images catalogue-ready. We are building agentic refinement on Claude: Claude is the agent that compares each AI image with the real product shot, finds the garment artifacts, and runs the retouch through our image tools until the garment matches. A person approves every final image.

1 · Find · Claude

Claude spots what the image model got wrong.

Claude's vision compares the AI image with the product shot and lists every garment artifact: warped prints, broken buttons, changed collars, wrong fabric texture, missing logos or stitching.

2 · Retouch · Claude

Claude plans the fix and runs it.

Using tool calls, Claude drives Headloom's retouching tools on each flagged area, then checks the result again and repeats until the garment matches the source.

3 · Approve · Human

A person signs off every final image.

The reviewer sees the before, the after and Claude's notes side by side, and approves, corrects or rejects. Nothing is exported without that approval.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Headloom?+

Headloom makes AI-generated fashion images catalogue-ready. It combines AI head swap with a Claude agent that compares each image with the real product shot, finds garment artifacts and retouches them, and a person approves every final image. The head-swap engine is in production; the Claude refinement agent is in pilot.

Can we try it on our own images?+

Yes. We run a scoped paid pilot on a sample of your shots, using identities you have the rights to, before any production commitment.

Who is behind Headloom?+

Headloom is built in Chennai, India, and led by co-founder Vishaal Ravi. See the Company page.

What happens to the images we send?+

Images are used only to produce your outputs. They are not used to train models for anyone else, and they are deleted at the end of the pilot or on request. See our Privacy Policy.

What comes after headswap?+

Agentic refinement on Claude, now in pilot, is next. After that we run it across full daily catalogue queues for fashion and ecommerce teams, inside the same review-gated workflow.

How fast can a project turn around?+

Turnaround depends on shot count, resolution, review cycles, and deployment model. We scope a reviewable pilot first, then agree production volumes and turnaround in the workspace plan.

Do we retain control over likeness rights?+

Yes. Work is performed only against approved assets and applicable likeness permissions. Commercial terms, usage rights, retention, and audit requirements are agreed before production.
