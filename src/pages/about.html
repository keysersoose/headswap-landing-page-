About Headloom

Headloom builds AI production tools for creative teams that work with high volumes of imagery. Our first product is a controlled AI head-swap workspace: an approved identity goes in, and a reviewed, high-resolution output comes out. Lighting, hair, occlusion and composition are all checked by a person before export.

How Headloom uses Claude

Headloom makes AI-generated fashion images catalogue-ready. Image models often get the garment wrong: warped prints, broken buttons, changed collars, fabric that looks fake. We are building Claude as the agent that fixes this. It compares each AI image with the real product shot, finds the garment artifacts, and runs the retouch through our image tools until the garment matches. A person approves every final image.

The Claude refinement agent is in pilot. Next, we want to run it on full catalogue volume in paid pilots, with sharper vision on fine garment detail like prints, stitching and logos, and reliable tool use across our retouching tools.
