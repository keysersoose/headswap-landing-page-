About Headloom

Headloom builds AI production tools for creative teams that work with high volumes of imagery. Our first product is a controlled AI head-swap workspace: an approved identity goes in, and a reviewed, high-resolution output comes out. Lighting, hair, occlusion and composition are all checked by a person before export.

How Headloom uses Claude

Headloom is a product built on Anthropic's Claude. In the head-swap workflow, Claude's vision model checks every generated image against its source and flags garment changes, identity drift, and lighting, hair or occlusion errors for the human reviewer. Claude also drafts product captions and image metadata for approved outputs. Both features are in pilot; a person still approves every image before export.

The same Claude-based checks are the foundation of our next products: tools that detect and fix AI hallucinations and artifacts in fashion and ecommerce images at production scale.
